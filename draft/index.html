PaintScout

⚠ ESTIMATES ARE NOT APPRAISALS
PaintScout helps one collector sort paintings. Its research results are estimates built from cited comparable sales. An appraisal is a formal valuation by a qualified professional, and this app never produces one. Your records stay on this device unless you send them.
